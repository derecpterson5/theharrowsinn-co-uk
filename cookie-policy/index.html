    What are cookies?

    Cookies are small text files saved on a device by a website, a browser or a service related to the website. On this website, for example, they might remember basic preferences such as language, display settings or whether a pop-up has already been displayed. They generally can't read all the information on your device. Nor are they files that make programs run like software does. Some cookies get deleted when you close the browser. Others remain a while longer, so the site can recognise your next visit. The difference is important because it changes how the website responds the next time it's opened.
